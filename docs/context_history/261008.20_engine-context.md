## 2. Cash Account Specifications & Regulatory Invariants

### 2.1 SEC Rule 15c6-1 (T+1 Settlement) & Capital Recycling
- The engine operates strictly within a **Cash Account** (no margin borrowing, no margin interest, no naked shorting).
- Under the SEC **T+1 settlement mandate**, equity transactions settle on the next business day ($T+1$).
- **Intraday Capital Recycling**:
  - Settled cash (Bucket 1) used to purchase shares converts into settled equity.
  - When shares bought with settled cash are sold on the same day, the proceeds land in **Unsettled Cash (Bucket 2)**.
  - **Bucket 2 proceeds can be immediately re-invested into new day trades on $T$ without penalty.**
  - However, selling any position that was funded using Bucket 2 cash *before* the original transaction settles on $T+1$ triggers a **Good Faith Violation (GFV)**.

### 2.2 Regulation T Good Faith Violation (GFV) Avoidance
- **Funding Provenance Tracking**: Every order in `SettlementLedger` tracks whether it was funded via settled cash (Bucket 1) or unsettled proceeds (Bucket 2).
- **GFV Hardware Gate**: In `execution/order_manager.py`, any sell order attempting to liquidate shares funded by unsettled cash is **unconditionally blocked** prior to $T+1$ settlement, even during mandatory end-of-day flatten routines.
- **Hard Cash Buffer**: Orders are blocked if Bucket 1 settled cash falls below $\$10.00$.

### 2.3 FINRA Rule 4210 Pattern Day Trader (PDT) Exemption
- FINRA Rule 4210 (PDT rule requiring $\$25,000$ minimum equity for $4+$ day trades in 5 business days) **strictly applies only to Margin Accounts**.
- **Cash Accounts are legally exempt from the PDT 4-trade restriction.**
- Day trading volume in this account is limited only by available settled cash and GFV rules, enabling unconstrained high-frequency day trading on sub-$\$25,000$ balances.

### 2.4 Macro Liquidity Invariant Math (Defect Fix Preserved)
$$\text{True Equity} = \text{settled\_cash} + \text{open\_positions\_market\_value}$$
$$\text{Total Liquid Backstop} = \text{True Equity} + \text{external\_liquid\_backstop}$$
- Cumulative gross closed turnover (Bucket 2 proceeds) is **never added** to settled cash in backstop calculations, eliminating phantom equity inflation.

### 2.5 SWVXX Sweep Advisory Firewall
- Money market sweeps into `SWVXX` require $T+1$ redemption cycles that choke morning day-trading float.
- In `core/liquidity_policy.py`:
  - `min_account_equity_usd = Decimal("25000.00")`
  - `operational_float_floor_usd = Decimal("3500.00")`
  - `_advise_sweep()` returns `action="NONE"` whenever account NLV is below $\$25,000$ or settled cash is below $\$3,500$.

### 3.1 Systemd Service Topology

1. **`schwab-trader.service`**:
   - `CPUAffinity=0`: Pinned strictly to CPU Core 0.
   - `Nice=-10`: Elevated priority above standard user processes.
   - `LimitMEMLOCK=infinity`: Prevents memory paging to disk.
   - `ExecStart=python main.py --headless-trader --live`.
   - Owns the exclusive Schwab streaming WebSocket connection.
   - Broadcasts real-time tick frames to `/tmp/schwab_trader.sock`.
   - Polls `state/control_signal.json` every 250ms for emergency HALT or LIQUIDATE commands.

2. **`schwab-web.service`**:
   - `CPUAffinity=1`: Pinned strictly to CPU Core 1.
   - `Nice=0`: Standard priority.
   - `ExecStart=python -m uvicorn api.server:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8080 --workers 1`.
   - Zero external connections to Schwab WebSocket streamer.
   - Connects to `/tmp/schwab_trader.sock` to relay ticks to dashboard browser WebSockets.
   - Queries `state/trades.db` via read-only URI connection (`file:state/trades.db?mode=ro`).
   - Writes emergency signals atomically to `state/control_signal.json`.

3. **`schwab-backfill.service` & `schwab-backfill.timer`**:
   - `CPUAffinity=2-3`, `Nice=19`, `IOSchedulingClass=idle`.
   - Scheduled at **17:15 EDT** weekdays (`workers/off_hours_backfill.py`).
   - Rate limited to 30 RPM (2.0s delay).
   - Aborts if daily API consumption $\ge 2,500$ calls.
   - Ingests missing 1m bars and generates 20-day ADV minute profiles for 24 symbols into `market_baselines.db`.

4. **`schwab-midday.service` & `schwab-midday.timer`**:
   - `CPUAffinity=2-3`, `Nice=19`, `IOSchedulingClass=idle`.
   - Scheduled at **11:35 EDT** weekdays during Midday Freeze (`workers/midday_optimizer.py`).
   - Evaluates 120 morning bars + 5-day sliding window ($N \approx 2,000$ bars).
   - Calculates Hurst Exponent ($H$) and Choppiness Index ($CI$). If $H \le 0.50$, writes overrides to `state/dynamic_policy.json` (Power Hour RVOL hurdle $1.65\times$, size scale $0.35\times$).

### 4.2 Inter-Process Communication (IPC)

1. **`state/control_signal.json`**:
   ```json
   {
     "sequence_id": 1728414120000,
     "timestamp": "2026-10-08T15:02:00.000Z",
   ```

## 5. Operational Schedule & Risk Boundaries

### 5.1 Five Intraday Trading Phases (Eastern Time)

| Phase | Hours (EDT) | Permitted Operations | Sizing Multiplier |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **PRE_MARKET** | 04:00 – 09:30 | History hydration, token refresh, baseline verification. No entries. | $0.0\times$ |
| **MORNING_DRIVE** | 09:30 – 10:15 | 15m ORB evaluation, High-RVOL trend breakouts, aggressive entries. | $1.0\times$ (Full) |
| **MID_MORNING** | 10:15 – 11:30 | VWAP mean-reversion, trend continuations. | $1.0\times$ (Full) |
| **MIDDAY_FREEZE** | 11:30 – 14:00 | **ENTRIES LOCKED**. Position management & trailing stops only. Midday optimizer runs at 11:35 EDT. | $0.0\times$ |
| **POWER_HOUR** | 14:00 – 15:35 | Secondary breakout entries. Dynamically throttled if morning was choppy. | $0.50\times$ (or dynamic $0.35\times$) |
| **PRE_CLOSE** | 15:35 – 15:50 | **ENTRIES LOCKED**. Normal trailing stop exits. | $0.0\times$ |
| **MANDATORY_FLATTEN**| 15:50 – 16:00 | **Unconditional position liquidation**. All managed inventory sold at market. | Liquidate |
| **POST_CLOSE** | 16:00 – 20:00 | End-of-day reconciliation. 17:15 EDT bar backfill and ADV curve sync. | $0.0\times$ |

### 5.2 Capital Conservation Clamps
- **Single-Ticker Exposure Cap**: Max notional per ticker = $33\%$ of account NLV.
- **Max Risk per Trade**: $2\%$ of account NLV.
- **Max Absolute Loss**: $\$50.00$ per trade (Quarter-Kelly bounded).
- **Daily Drawdown Circuit Breaker**: $5\%$ of NLV. If breached, all entries are halted for the day.
- **Tier 2 Catastrophe Stop**: Broker-side stop at $4.0\%$ below fill price placed immediately upon execution.
