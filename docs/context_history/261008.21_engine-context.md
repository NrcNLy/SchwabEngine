### 2.1 SEC Rule 15c6-1 (T+1 Settlement Mandate) & Capital Recycling Mechanics
- **Account Type Mandate**: The engine executes strictly within a **Cash Account** (no margin borrowing, no negative cash balances, no naked shorting).
- **Settlement Timeline**: Under SEC Rule 15c6-1, all equities and ETFs settle on $T+1$ (the next business day following execution).
- **Intraday Capital Recycling**:
  - Settled cash (Bucket 1) used to purchase shares is fully deployed into equity positions.
  - When shares originally bought with settled cash are liquidated on the same day ($T$), the gross proceeds immediately land in **Unsettled Cash (Bucket 2)**.
  - **Bucket 2 funds are fully eligible to fund new long purchases on the same trading day ($T$) without penalty.**
  - This recycling mechanism enables multiple iterations of trading volume throughout the session using the same core capital base.

### 2.2 Regulation T Good Faith Violation (GFV) Hardware Blocks
- **The GFV Trap**: Under Federal Reserve Regulation T, purchasing a security with unsettled funds (Bucket 2) and subsequently selling that same security *before* the original funding transaction settles on $T+1$ constitutes a **Good Faith Violation (GFV)**. Three GFVs within a 12-month period forces a 90-day cash-up-front freeze.
- **Funding Provenance Tracking**: `SettlementLedger` tracks every open position lot by funding provenance:
  - `settled_funded_lots`: Positions entered using Bucket 1 settled cash.
  - `unsettled_funded_lots`: Positions entered using Bucket 2 recycled proceeds.
- **Hardware GFV Gate (`execution/order_manager.py`)**:
  - Any sell order attempting to liquidate an `unsettled_funded` position lot prior to $T+1$ settlement is **unconditionally rejected and blocked**.
  - This block supersedes all standard strategy signals and even overrides the mandatory end-of-day 15:50 EDT liquidation routine.
- **Cash Reserve Floor**: Purchases are rejected if Bucket 1 settled cash drops below $\$10.00$.

### 2.3 FINRA Rule 4210 Pattern Day Trader (PDT) Exemption
- **Regulatory Scope**: FINRA Rule 4210 defines a Pattern Day Trader as any account executing 4 or more day trades within 5 rolling business days, requiring a mandatory $\$25,000$ equity minimum.
- **Cash Account Exemption**: **FINRA Rule 4210 strictly applies only to Margin Accounts.**
- **Operational Reality**: Cash accounts are **legally and structurally exempt from the 4-day-trade limit**. Day-trading frequency is bounded strictly by available settled cash and GFV compliance, enabling unconstrained high-frequency day-trading operations even on accounts with net liquidity below $\$25,000$.

### 2.4 Macro Liquidity Invariant Math
To prevent phantom liquidity expansion and accounting double-counting:

$$\text{True Equity} = \text{settled\_cash} + \text{open\_positions\_market\_value}$$

$$\text{Total Liquid Backstop} = \text{True Equity} + \text{external\_liquid\_backstop}$$

- **Non-Additive Turnover**: Cumulative gross sales turnover (unsettled Bucket 2 proceeds) is **strictly non-additive** to True Equity or Total Liquid Backstop. Gross turnover represents re-routed purchasing power, not net asset expansion.
- Synchronized across `core/liquidity_models.py`, `core/collateral_engine.py`, and `api/server.py`.

### 2.5 SWVXX Sweep Advisory Firewall
- Sweep transactions into Charles Schwab Value Advantage Money Market (`SWVXX`) require an overnight $T+1$ redemption turnaround that freezes morning liquidity.
- In `core/liquidity_policy.py`:
  - `min_account_equity_usd = Decimal("25000.00")`
  - `operational_float_floor_usd = Decimal("3500.00")`
  - `_advise_sweep()` returns `action="NONE"` whenever total account equity is below $\$25,000$ or settled operational cash is below $\$3,500$.

## 6. Real-Time Synthetic Divergence Engine (`core/synthetic_divergence.py`)

The Synthetic Divergence Engine monitors inverse pairs in real time using rolling 15-second circular buffers to detect institutional volume absorption traps and prevent false breakout fills.

### 6.1 Tracked Synthetic Pairs
- `SOXL` $\longleftrightarrow$ `SOXS` (Semiconductor 3x)
- `TQQQ` $\longleftrightarrow$ `SQQQ` (Nasdaq 3x)
- `TNA` $\longleftrightarrow$ `TZA` (Russell 2000 3x)
- `UCO` $\longleftrightarrow$ `SCO` (Crude Oil 2x)

### 6.2 Mathematical Formulas

#### 1. Relative Volume Delta Spread
$$\Delta V_{\text{spread}} = \left( \frac{V_{\text{Bull}, t}}{\overline{V}_{\text{Bull}}} \right) - \left( \frac{V_{\text{Bear}, t}}{\overline{V}_{\text{Bear}}} \right) = \text{RVOL}_{\text{Bull}} - \text{RVOL}_{\text{Bear}}$$

#### 2. Normalized Synthetic Price Product
$$S_t = \frac{P_{\text{Bull}, t} \cdot P_{\text{Bear}, t}}{P_{\text{Bull}, 0} \cdot P_{\text{Bear}, 0}}$$
Under pure symmetric price movement without volatility decay or tracking error divergence, $S_t \approx 1.0$.

#### 3. Liquidity Absorption Trap Detection & Guard
A market maker liquidity trap occurs when heavy volume is absorbed without directional price expansion.
- **Trigger Condition (`LIQUIDITY_TRAP_ACTIVE`)**:
  1. Both legs show simultaneously elevated volume:
     $$\text{RVOL}_{\text{Bull}} \ge 1.30 \quad \text{and} \quad \text{RVOL}_{\text{Bear}} \ge 1.30$$
  2. Absolute price velocity across both legs is compressed:
     $$\vert{}\Delta P_{\text{Bull}}\vert{} < 0.15\% \quad \text{and} \quad \vert{}\Delta P_{\text{Bear}}\vert{} < 0.15\%$$
- **120-Second Cooldown Enforcement**: Once triggered, `is_trap_active()` remains `True` for **120 seconds** after conditions subside.
- **Entry Suppression**: Suppresses long entries across `core/universe_manager.py` and `execution/strategies.py`, preventing whipsaw losses in false breakouts.
