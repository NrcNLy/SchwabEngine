### 1.1 Synthetic Divergence Engine (`core/synthetic_divergence.py`)
Tracks 4 canonical inverse leveraged ETF pairs to identify microstructural imbalance and false liquidity traps:

#### A. 09:30:00 EDT Cash-Open Anchor Latching & Pre-Market Suppression
* **Pre-Market Invariant ($t < \text{09:30:00 EDT}$):** Opening baseline price anchors ($P_{\text{Bull},0}$, $P_{\text{Bear},0}$) are **never latched** during pre-market streaming ticks ($t < \text{09:30:00 EDT}$). Pre-market ticks populate rolling 15-second tick buffers for velocity monitoring but preserve `p_bull_0 = None` and `p_bear_0 = None`.
* **Cash-Open Boundary Crossing:** When `record_tick()` detects transition from pre-market ($\text{last\_tick} < \text{09:30:00}$) to regular trading hours ($\text{current\_tick} \ge \text{09:30:00}$), it calls `reset_opening_anchors()`.
* **Latching Logic (`reset_opening_anchors()` & `record_tick()`):**
  ```python
  def reset_opening_anchors(self) -> None:
      with self._lock:
          self._initial_prices.clear()
          for pair_key, state in self.pairs.items():
              state.p_bull_0 = None
              state.p_bear_0 = None
  ```
  On the first regular session tick ($t \ge \text{09:30:00 EDT}$), the engine anchors initial prices:
  ```python
  if in_regular_hours:
      if sym not in self._initial_prices or self._initial_prices[sym] <= 0:
          self._initial_prices[sym] = price
      pair = self._symbol_to_pair.get(sym)
      if pair is not None:
          if sym == pair.bull_symbol.upper() and (pair.p_bull_0 is None or pair.p_bull_0 <= 0):
              pair.p_bull_0 = price
          elif sym == pair.bear_symbol.upper() and (pair.p_bear_0 is None or pair.p_bear_0 <= 0):
              pair.p_bear_0 = price
  ```

#### B. Relative Volume Delta Spread & Normalized Synthetic Price Product
* **Relative Volume (RVOL):** Evaluated over a rolling 15-second circular buffer ($W = 15\,\text{s}$) against expected 15-second baseline volume ($\overline{V}_{\text{sym}}$):
  $$\text{RVOL}_{\text{Bull}, t} = \frac{V_{\text{Bull}, t}(15\text{s})}{\overline{V}_{\text{Bull}}}, \quad \text{RVOL}_{\text{Bear}, t} = \frac{V_{\text{Bear}, t}(15\text{s})}{\overline{V}_{\text{Bear}}}$$
* **Relative Volume Delta Spread ($\Delta V_{\text{spread}}$):**
  $$\Delta V_{\text{spread}} = \text{RVOL}_{\text{Bull}, t} - \text{RVOL}_{\text{Bear}, t}$$
* **Normalized Synthetic Price Product ($S_t$):**
  $$S_t = \frac{P_{\text{Bull}, t} \cdot P_{\text{Bear}, t}}{P_{\text{Bull}, 0} \cdot P_{\text{Bear}, 0}}$$
  Under ideal zero-slippage inverse leverage replication, $S_t \approx 1.0$. Severe deviations indicate volatility decay, skew, or market maker spread widening.

#### C. Dual-Leg Volume Absorption Trap Detection
Detects market maker inventory churn without directional price expansion (false breakouts / liquidity traps).
* **Trap Condition Formulation:**
  Flags `LIQUIDITY_TRAP_ACTIVE` for a pair when both legs exhibit simultaneous volume expansion accompanied by tight price compression:
  1. $\text{RVOL}_{\text{Bull}} \ge 1.30 \quad \text{and} \quad \text{RVOL}_{\text{Bear}} \ge 1.30$
  2. $|\Delta P_{\text{Bull}}| < 0.15\% \quad \text{and} \quad |\Delta P_{\text{Bear}}| < 0.15\%$
  where price velocity over the 15-second buffer is:
  $$\Delta P = \frac{P_{\text{latest}} - P_{\text{start}}}{P_{\text{start}}} \times 100.0$$
* **120-Second Cooldown Persistence:**
  Once conditions are met, `_last_trap_condition_time[pair.name]` is updated. The trap remains active for a full 120.0 seconds after the condition subsides:
  $$\Delta t_{\text{elapsed}} = t - t_{\text{last\_trap}} < 120.0\,\text{s} \implies \text{TRAP ACTIVE}$$
* **Engine Action:** Emits `LIQUIDITY_TRAP_SUPPRESSION` event to `telemetry.db` and enforces 100% cash stay (vetoes candidate entry orders).

### 1.2 Cross-Asset Ranking & Signal Dispatching (`core/universe_manager.py` & `execution/strategies.py`)

#### A. Composite Momentum Score Formula
Candidate instruments are ranked to prioritize primary trading vehicles over secondary correlated assets:
$$S_i = 0.50 \cdot Z_{\text{mom}, i} + 0.30 \cdot \left(\frac{\text{RSI}_{14, i} - 50.0}{50.0}\right) + 0.20 \cdot \left(\frac{\text{ADX}_{14, i}}{100.0}\right)$$
Where:
* $Z_{\text{mom}, i} = \frac{P_{t, i} - \mu_{P, 20, i}}{\sigma_{P, 20, i}}$ (20-day rolling price momentum Z-score)
* $\text{RSI}_{14, i}$ is the 14-period Wilder Relative Strength Index normalized to $[-1.0, +1.0]$
* $\text{ADX}_{14, i}$ is the 14-period Average Directional Index normalized to $[0.0, 1.0]$

#### B. Priority Allocation vs. Greedy FIFO Dispatching
* **Anti-Starvation Mechanism:** To prevent lower-conviction or secondary ETFs from consuming limited settled cash via greedy first-in, first-out (FIFO) execution, `UniverseManager.dispatch_candidate_signals()` and `StrategyEngine.rank_candidate_signals()` sort all candidate signals descending by $S_i$:
  $$\mathbb{U}_{\text{approved}} = \text{sort}\big(\text{signals}, \text{key} = \lambda \, \text{sig}: S_i(\text{sig.symbol}), \text{reverse} = \text{True}\big)$$
* **Settled Cash Clamping:**
  ```python
  avail_cash = float(ledger.settled_cash) - float(ledger.cash_buffer)
  if (sig.entry_price * sig.quantity) > avail_cash:
      max_by_cash = int(avail_cash // float(sig.entry_price))
      sig = self.clamp_signal_size(sig, max_by_cash)
  ```
  Higher-momentum primary assets (`SOXL`, `TQQQ`, `TNA`) receive full capital allocation first; subsequent candidates are clamped or starved cleanly without race conditions.

### 1.3 Wash-Sale Audit Logic & Boundary Scope (`core/universe_mask.py` & `execution/order_manager.py`)

#### A. Wash-Sale Exclusion Invariant (IRC §1091)
* **Target Scope:** Designed strictly to prevent day-trading lots from contaminating **active external swing holdings**.
* **Audit Rules (`UniverseExclusionMask.audit_holdings_for_wash_sale()`):**
  A symbol is quarantined if and only if:
  1. An active broker portfolio position has an origination timestamp prior to the current session open:
     $$t_{\text{lot}} < \text{Session}_{\text{open}}$$
  2. The position is explicitly marked `external_schwab == True` (discretionary multi-day swing tranche).
* **Zero 30-Day Closed-Loss Lockouts:**
  The engine **does NOT query `trades.db` for closed losses incurred over the past 30 days or prior sessions**. Intraday trading losses incurred and liquidated in prior sessions **never** quarantine symbols from same-day or next-day intraday execution.
* **Intraday Loss Circuit Breakers:**
  Single-session circuit breakers halt intraday entries if daily realized losses hit or exceed $-\$50.00$ (or $-3.0\%$ portfolio NLV), protecting capital without retroactive tax locking.

#### Strategy 1: 15-Minute Opening Range Breakout (15m ORB)
* **Eligibility & Time Window:** Active during `MORNING_DRIVE` (09:45:00 EDT to 10:30:00 EDT). Opening Range high ($H_{\text{orb}}$) and low ($L_{\text{orb}}$) established across 09:30:00–09:45:00 EDT.
* **Regime Gate:** Choppiness Index $\text{CI}_{14} < 38.2$ (Regime A: Trend Expansion), $\text{VWAP Slope} > 0^\circ$ (accumulation), $\text{RVOL} \ge 1.50$.
* **Entry Trigger:** 1-minute close crossing above 15m range high:
  $$\text{Close}_t > H_{\text{orb}}$$
* **Stop Placement:** Initial Tier-1 stop set at ORB midpoint or $2 \times \text{ATR}_{14}$ below entry:
  $$P_{\text{stop}} = \max\left(\frac{H_{\text{orb}} + L_{\text{orb}}}{2},\; P_{\text{entry}} - 2.0 \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right)$$
* **Profit Target:** Fixed risk-reward multiple:
  $$P_{\text{target}} = P_{\text{entry}} + 2.5 \cdot (P_{\text{entry}} - P_{\text{stop}})$$
* **Hurst-Gated Trailing Ratchet:**
  Trailing stop distance derived from Yang-Zhang volatility:
  $$d_{\text{stop}} = \max\left(P_{\text{HWM}} \cdot 2.0 \cdot \sigma_{\text{yz}} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{1/390}{252}},\; 0.005 \cdot P_{\text{HWM}}\right)$$
  Stop ratchets higher monotonically with High-Water Mark ($P_{\text{HWM}}$) **only if** Hurst exponent $H > 0.55$. If $H \le 0.50$ (mean-reverting noise), the ratchet is locked to prevent stop-hunting whipsaws.

#### A. Bayesian Quarter-Kelly Position Sizing
* **Base Formula:**
  $$\text{Shares}_{\text{raw}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Risk Capital}}{|P_{\text{entry}} - P_{\text{stop}}|} \right\rfloor$$
  Where maximum trade dollar risk is hard-capped at $\$10.00$ for base signals, and overall session trade loss is bounded at $\$50.00$.
* **Macro Regime Scaling:** Multiplied by macroeconomic and LLM sentiment conviction factor $\mu_{\text{macro}} \in [0.50, 1.00]$.
* **Mathematical Stop Floor:**
  $$d_{\text{stop}} \ge 0.005 \cdot P_{\text{entry}} \quad (50\text{ basis points})$$
  Under no circumstances can any strategy place a stop tighter than $0.50\%$ from entry, protecting against spread noise collapse.

#### B. Portfolio Circuit Breakers
* **Max Loss Cap Per Trade:** $\$50.00$ absolute loss ceiling.
* **Daily Drawdown Limit:** $-3.0\%$ of starting session NLV ($-\$113.19$). Breaching this halts all subsequent entries immediately and triggers emergency de-risking.

#### D. Regulatory Cash Account Compliance (SEC Rule 15c6-1 & Reg T)
* **T+1 Settlement Lifecycle:** Trades settle on the next business day ($T+1$).
* **Hardware Good Faith Violation (GFV) Block:**
  The engine separates capital into:
  * **Bucket 1 (Settled Cash):** Only funds in Bucket 1 can be allocated to buys.
